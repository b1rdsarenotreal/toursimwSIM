Auto-Fill Field from Rankings

Pick a ranking week to pull the field from, and (optionally) a different week to order the seeds by. Auto-fill only sets seeds/entrants that are currently empty — it won't overwrite anyone you've already placed by hand.

Entry List

Build the actual list of players who entered — mark any wild cards, then process it using the two dates above. Direct main-draw acceptances and qualifying acceptances are cut off by rank (as of the entry-list date) after wild cards are set aside; seeds among the main-draw field are then ordered using the seeding date. This only fills empty seed/entrant slots — anyone already placed by hand stays put.

Qualifying Setup

Qualifying Entrants

Search and add players below.

Seeding

Assign seed 1 through seed . Seeds 1 and 2 anchor opposite ends of the draw; the rest are drawn randomly within their pot (3–4, 5–8, 9–16…) so same-pot seeds can't meet early. Byes go to the top seeds automatically.

Unseeded Entrants

Everyone else in the field. They'll be drawn at random into the remaining slots.

Manual Slot Overrides

Fine-tune individual slots directly — useful for fixing a single slot without redrawing the whole bracket.

Historical Slam Record

For appearances from before this tracker existed, or from outside this system entirely. These are purely additive — added on top of whatever's actually recorded here, and they never affect rankings or points, only what shows on this page. Add one row per major — it'll count toward both that major's own filter and Total.

Retire

They'll drop off the rankings from this date onward, but stay exactly as they were in any week's rankings before it — nothing about their past results changes.

Add Bye Week

Marks a week where no tournament is played. Rankings still "publish" for that week — old results keep aging out of the 52-week window, and the week becomes selectable for seeding a future draw — just nothing new gets added.

Bulk Add Players

One player per line: Name, COU. The country code is optional but powers the flag — use a 2 or 3-letter code (e.g. ESP, KEN, JPN, US).

Add Tournament

WATP Finals is a fixed 8-player, 2-group round robin plus a 4-player knockout — draw size and qualifying don't apply. You'll assign the two groups from the tournament's own page.
